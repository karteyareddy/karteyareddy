The part of AI I like most comes after the notebook: wiring a model into something a person can open and trust. That has meant a knee-MRI classifier with explainable heatmaps, a sign-recognition model that knows when to defer to a human interpreter, a traffic optimiser that has to cope with fog and flooding, and a care app that families use every day.

My work sits where **computer vision, language and learning systems** meet **full-stack engineering**: Python and PyTorch for the models, TypeScript and Next.js for the product, Supabase or PostgreSQL underneath, and lately Qiskit when a problem is worth a quantum detour.

<a href="https://github.com/karteyareddy/Q-TrafficX"><picture><source media="(max-width: 700px)" srcset="assets/sp-p-qtrafficx-m-auto.svg"><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/sp-p-qtrafficx-light.svg"><img src="assets/sp-p-qtrafficx.svg" width="100%" alt="Q-TrafficX: weather-aware traffic digital twin optimised with hybrid QAOA on Qiskit Aer" /></picture></a>

A hybrid quantum-classical traffic platform for a 6-intersection network. Rain, flooding, fog, accidents and emergency vehicles reshape demand, and the system rebuilds signal timing and routing as QUBO problems on each update, solved with QAOA on Qiskit Aer and refined classically. Real-time monitoring, flood/fog awareness, an interactive 2D/3D digital twin, and 181 passing tests. Top 30 at the Quantexa Hackathon.
**[Repository →](https://github.com/karteyareddy/Q-TrafficX)**

<picture><source media="(max-width: 700px)" srcset="assets/sp-p-scanoraai-m-auto.svg"><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/sp-p-scanoraai-light.svg"><img src="assets/sp-p-scanoraai.svg" width="100%" alt="ScanoraAI: knee-MRI screening with a ResNet18 multi-label classifier and Grad-CAM explainability" /></picture>

A multi-modal screening platform I co-developed: camera-based kinematic analysis plus a ResNet18 classifier for knee-MRI pathology. The model handles 12 labels at once and reached **94.1% binary accuracy, 86.5% macro F1 and 96.8% mean ROC-AUC** across the 10 evaluated classes. Grad-CAM heatmaps show which regions drove each prediction, and a Streamlit interface prepares AI-assisted reports for a clinician to review.
*Repository not public yet.*

<a href="https://github.com/karteyareddy/ISL"><picture><source media="(max-width: 700px)" srcset="assets/sp-p-signbridge-m-auto.svg"><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/sp-p-signbridge-light.svg"><img src="assets/sp-p-signbridge.svg" width="100%" alt="SignBridge India: Deaf-first Indian Sign Language accessibility network" /></picture></a>

An accessibility network for hospitals, stations and service counters. A QR code at the counter opens phrase cards and offline service packs with no install. Hand and pose landmarks are extracted in the browser with MediaPipe, so frames never leave the device. A FastAPI model only answers above an 85% confidence threshold; below it, the user is routed to visual phrase selection or a human interpreter.
**[Repository →](https://github.com/karteyareddy/ISL)**

<a href="https://bed-care-connect.vercel.app"><picture><source media="(max-width: 700px)" srcset="assets/sp-p-bedcare-m-auto.svg"><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/sp-p-bedcare-light.svg"><img src="assets/sp-p-bedcare.svg" width="100%" alt="BedCare Connect: structured care management for bedridden patients with Gemini case-sheet summaries" /></picture></a>

Families and caretakers of bedridden patients get structured task scheduling, real-time dashboards and role-based access, while Gemini reads case sheets and summarises medical history for whoever is on shift. Built with Next.js and Supabase, and packaged for Android with Capacitor.
**[Live app →](https://bed-care-connect.vercel.app)** · **[Repository →](https://github.com/karteyareddy/BedCare-Connect)**
